QA Systems

Quality from Source to Patient

Our multi-layered quality system ensures that every batch that leaves our facilities meets or exceeds international pharmacopoeial standards.

Incoming Material Testing

Every raw material and excipient undergoes rigorous identity, purity, and potency testing before release to manufacturing.

  • Vendor qualification audit
  • CoA verification
  • Identity testing by HPLC/GC
  • Microbial testing
  • Release to manufacturing

In-Process Quality Control

Real-time monitoring during production ensures every batch meets defined parameters.

  • Weight variation checks
  • Dissolution testing
  • Blend uniformity
  • Coating weight gain
  • Leak testing for injectables

Finished Product Testing

Comprehensive batch release testing includes physical, chemical, microbiological, and stability assessments.

  • Assay & related substances
  • Dissolution profile
  • Sterility testing
  • Particulate matter analysis
  • Container closure integrity
